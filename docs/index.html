How the numbers work

Lineup
Five players on the floor together for one team. Every stretch of a game where the same five stayed on the court counts toward it.
Net rating
Points the lineup scored per 100 possessions (Off) minus points it allowed per 100 possessions (Def). +10 means it outscored opponents by 10 points every 100 trips. Garbage time is included.
Minimums
A small sample can make any five look great, so each list needs a number of possessions, shown above it. For the season in progress the minimum grows with the games played, so early lists fill up and later ones tighten.
Duos
Two players, counted whenever both were in the same five, whoever the other three were.
Hottest lineups
The last seven days with games. If fewer than 5 lineups reach 30 possessions, the window widens to 14 days, then to the season.
Records
Win-loss records are added up from the same play-by-play, overtime included.
Source
Lineup data from Ramiro Bentes (@NbaInRstats), built from NBA play-by-play. Regular season only. Season 1996-97 onward. Updated every day of the season.
